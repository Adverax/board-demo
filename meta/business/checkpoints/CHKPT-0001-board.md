## What we're building

A small kanban board: a Go backend and one web page. A team keeps its work items on
three fixed columns (todo, doing, done), adds, edits, deletes and moves cards one
column at a time. There is one shared board, and everyone sees the same state.

## Key trade-offs

- `doing` is capped at 3 cards; a move into a full `doing` is refused, not queued.
- Moves are one column at a time, so a card cannot jump from todo straight to done.
- Storage is in memory: the board is lost on restart (accepted for the demo).
- No authentication, users or multiple boards; archive and drag and drop are deferred.
